HEON — PART 12: HELP CENTER, CUSTOMER SUPPORT, BUG REPORTING, PLAYER FEEDBACK, AND TRUST & SAFETY

Extend HEON with a support experience that is honest, privacy-conscious, and backed by real storage or clearly identified as unavailable.

HELP CENTER
Provide searchable help topics, categories, troubleshooting steps, FAQs, contact options, and links to actual policy pages. Include empty, loading, and error states. Do not invent official contact addresses, service-level guarantees, or support hours.

SUPPORT TICKETS
Where a backend is configured, allow authenticated users to submit a ticket with category, subject, description, priority only if appropriate, optional permitted attachments, and a ticket reference generated by the server. Provide status history and replies. Enforce ticket ownership and restrict staff tools to authorized roles. Do not claim that a ticket was delivered or reviewed unless the server confirms it.

BUG REPORTING
Collect a concise description, steps to reproduce, expected versus actual behavior, app version, device/browser information only with appropriate notice and consent, and optional screenshots. Exclude passwords, access tokens, payment credentials, and unnecessary personal information. Let users preview diagnostic data before sending.

FEEDBACK AND SAFETY
Provide optional product feedback and report/block flows for harmful content or abuse. Store reports securely, restrict access, track actual status, and audit moderator actions. Avoid exposing reporter identity to the reported user unless a legitimate, reviewed process requires it.

ATTACHMENTS AND RETENTION
Validate file type and size, scan uploads where infrastructure supports it, use private storage, and issue authorized access links. Define retention and deletion rules. Do not allow uploaded content to execute as code.

TESTS
Test ticket ownership, staff permissions, invalid attachments, rate limits, spam, network failures, privacy redaction, status transitions, and notification behavior. Label unavailable backend functionality clearly and never display fabricated ticket records.
