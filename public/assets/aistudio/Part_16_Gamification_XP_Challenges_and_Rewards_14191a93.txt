HEON — PART 16: GAMIFICATION, XP, PLAYER LEVELS, CHALLENGES, REWARDS, AND SEASONAL EVENTS

Continue the HEON project with optional, fair gamification. Avoid gambling mechanics, paid random rewards, pay-to-win advantages, manipulative streak pressure, or deceptive reward promises.

XP AND LEVELS
Define transparent XP sources, level thresholds, maximums if applicable, and event rules. Award XP from verified server-side events or trusted integrations. Use idempotency keys and abuse limits to prevent duplicate claims. Keep the calculation in a central service and show the reason for each award.

CHALLENGES
Support daily, weekly, and longer-term challenges with clear eligibility, objective, progress, start/end times, completion criteria, and rewards. Reset schedules must use a documented timezone strategy. Prevent clients from self-reporting unverified completion. Show unavailable or expired states clearly and avoid punishing users for missed days.

REWARDS AND INVENTORY
Provide a catalog of earned cosmetics, profile decorations, badges, and other non-harmful rewards. Record claims and inventory on the backend. Never grant a reward twice because of retries. Explain whether rewards are earned, limited-time, or purchased, and keep paid products separate from random chance. Do not claim an item is owned until the server confirms it.

SEASONS AND ADMIN
Allow staff to configure seasonal events, challenges, eligibility, reward definitions, and publication dates through protected admin APIs. Record edits and avoid retroactively changing earned rewards without a clear policy. Display actual status and progress.

ANTI-ABUSE AND PRIVACY
Rate-limit claims, validate timestamps and event signatures where possible, detect duplicate events, and protect leaderboard integrity. Collect only the data required. Make participation optional and provide accessible alternatives to animation-heavy reward screens.

TESTS
Test duplicate claims, expired challenges, timezone boundaries, offline retries, invalid progress, reward revocation, season transitions, and admin permissions. Report real integrations and any mock data honestly.
